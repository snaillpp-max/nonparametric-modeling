# Appendix A Pulsating sphere implementation

The benchmark is a force-driven breathing sphere coupled to linear spherical radiation. The sphere radius is a=0.05 m, the effective structural mass is M=0.02 kg, and the uncoupled natural frequency is f0=1000 Hz. The structural damping ratio is 0.01, giving K=M omega0^2=789568.352087 N/m and B=2 zeta M omega0=2.51327412287 N s/m, where omega0=2 pi f0. The fluid density and sound speed are 1.204 kg/m^3 and 343 m/s. The reference force is Fref=0.001 N. Displacement, velocity and pressure are scaled by Xref=Fref/K, Vref=omega0 Xref and Pref=rho c Vref=0.003286326758 Pa, respectively.

Reference training trajectories are generated from the coupled structural and radial acoustic FEM equations. The acoustic variable is psi=r phi, where phi is the velocity potential; its normalized coordinate is y=psi/(a Vref lambda0), with lambda0=c/f0=0.343 m. The radial domain 0<=(r-a)/lambda0<=4 uses 64 quadratic elements, producing 129 acoustic nodes and 130 second-order coordinates when the structural displacement is included. The outer condition is the exact outgoing-wave condition for spherical waves. For the specified smooth harmonic force, the semidiscrete FEM response is evaluated through its matrix exponential representation from zero coordinates and velocities. The forcing envelope is [1-exp(-Omega t/(2 pi R))]^4, multiplying sin(Omega t+phase), where R is the onset constant in excitation periods.

Training uses four frequency ratios, 0.90, 0.96, 1.04 and 1.10, two phases, 0 and pi/2, and two force-amplitude ratios, 0.6 and 1.2. Eight unit-amplitude FEM histories are scaled to obtain these 16 conditions, exploiting the linearity of this benchmark. Each history spans 36 reference periods at 256 intervals per reference period, with R=0.2. Mass-weighted POD is applied to the joint structural-displacement and acoustic-potential snapshots without mean centering, using only training histories. The 24 retained modes satisfy U.T M U=I. Modal velocity and acceleration labels are obtained by five-point fourth-order differences. Sampling every fourth eligible point gives 36864 training pairs. Two separate validation conditions, at frequency ratios 0.93 and 1.07, amplitude ratio 0.9, R=0.35 and phase 0.4 rad, provide 4608 validation pairs.

The network has 49 inputs, two fixed Tanh hidden layers of widths 128 and 64, and 24 acceleration outputs. Training RMS scaling and random-feature seed 20260912 are used. Output ridge regression is followed, where needed, by an offline correction of unstable Schur directions of the learned zero-state Jacobian. This correction is incorporated into the output weights and does not insert FEM operator coefficients. The original selected feature scale and ridge ratio are 0.001 and 1e-12. Independent neural integration uses DOP853 with relative tolerance 2e-8, absolute tolerance 1e-11 and maximum step 0.2 in tau=omega0 t. Pressure is reconstructed from modal velocities and the acoustic-potential basis.

## Public example scope

The public `paper` profile preserves these settings and provides one prediction case, omitting the convergence audit and 50-frequency evaluation. Its `smoke` profile changes the mesh to eight elements, POD rank to eight, sampling to 64 intervals per reference period, duration to four reference periods, and training and model-selection grids to smaller sets. Smoke metrics establish pipeline execution only, not manuscript accuracy. Model-selection results are recorded separately.
